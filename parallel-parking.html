1Signal right · line up

Pull alongside the front car, about 1 m away, until your rear bumpers line up. Stop.

2Wheel full right · reverse

While stopped, turn the wheel all the way right. Back up slowly until the car sits at about 45°. Stop.

3Wheel straight · reverse

Straighten the wheel. Back up until your front bumper clears the car in front. Stop.

4Wheel full left · reverse

Turn the wheel all the way left. Back up until you are parallel to the curb. Stop.

5Wheel straight · centre

Straighten the wheel and pull forward to centre the car in the space.
