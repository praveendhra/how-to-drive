1Signal right · pull over

Pull as close to the right curb as you can. Stop. Check mirrors and blind spot.

2Signal left · turn wheel first

While stopped, turn the wheel all the way left. Then creep forward until your front bumper is near the opposite curb. Stop.

3Reverse · turn wheel first

While stopped, turn the wheel all the way right. Then back up slowly as far as you safely can toward the near curb. Stop.

4Drive · turn wheel first

While stopped, turn the wheel left. Then drive forward into the centre of your lane, straighten out, and go.
